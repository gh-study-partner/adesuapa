Adesua Pa - real voice recordings (optional)

The study timer speaks English with an African female voice found on the student's phone.
For a consistent voice on every phone, have a lady record these two lines and save them here as MP3:

  start.mp3   plays when a student taps START on the study timer
  pomo.mp3    plays when a 25-minute focus session finishes

Scripts:
  start.mp3 : Please turn on Do Not Disturb mode, or silence your notifications, for deep focus!
  pomo.mp3  : Pomodoro complete. Great work! Take a five minute break.

Keep each file under 10 seconds and under 300 KB. Upload the folder to GitHub with the rest of the site.
