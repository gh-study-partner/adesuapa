Adesua Pa - real voice recordings (optional)

If a lady who speaks Twi records these two lines, save them with EXACTLY these names in this folder
and upload to GitHub. The app will then play the recording instead of the phone's speech engine.

  start.mp3   plays when a student taps START on the study timer
  pomo.mp3    plays when a 25-minute focus session finishes

Scripts (please let a native Twi speaker check and correct the wording before recording):

  start.mp3 : Akwaaba! Mesre wo, ma wo fon no nye komm, na fa w'adwene to w'adesua so.
              (Welcome! Please silence your phone and focus on your study.)
  pomo.mp3  : Aye adwuma papa! Wo bere no awie. Gye w'ahome simma anum.
              (Well done! Your time is up. Take a five-minute rest.)

Keep each file short (under 10 seconds) and under 300 KB.
